# Relabelling terms ("Make it yours")

Clubs, seed libraries, societies, schools and businesses may offer their own version of Lineage Tracker, free of charge, using the official kit at https://lineagetracker.org/partners.html.

## What you can change

Using the kit only: your version's name, your logo, your colours, a welcome message, a link to your website, an optional variety list, and which features and modes are shown.

## Conditions

1. **Use the kit.** Relabelled versions run from the official Lineage Tracker app. You may not copy, host or modify the code.
2. **Keep the credit.** Your version shows "Powered by Lineage Tracker", and things people share from it say so. Don't hide or contradict it.
3. **Keep the support links.** Donation and support links go to Lineage Tracker. You may ask your members to support your own organisation separately.
4. **Don't charge for it.** You may not charge your members or customers for access to your version.
5. **Use your own name and logo.** Only use names, logos and variety lists you have the right to use. Don't use names or branding that suggest you are Lineage Tracker.
6. **Respect people's privacy.** Your members' records stay on their own devices. Don't ask them to send you their records unless they choose to.
7. **You're responsible for what you add.** That includes your welcome message, your variety list and any claims about your products.
8. **Withdrawal.** Lineage Tracker may withdraw a relabelled version that breaks these terms or is misleading or offensive. People using it keep their records and continue with plain Lineage Tracker.
